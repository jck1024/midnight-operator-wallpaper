# Midnight Operator v1.0 — laptop acceptance checklist

**Important:** These are pending checks for your Wallpaper Engine installation, not automatic passes. Keep v0.4 installed until v1.0 passes.

- [ ] Imported v1.0 under a new name; v0.4 is still available.
- [ ] City artwork, desktop icons, taskbar and widgets are displayed correctly at my laptop's native resolution.
- [ ] World Clock time for London and Hong Kong is correct; calendar highlights today's UK date.
- [ ] Countdown appears and counts toward the configured date; hides when toggled off.
- [ ] Spotify track metadata and album art update with playback.
- [ ] Spotify audio bars react at my normal 30–50% listening-volume test and settle during silence.
- [ ] All three dashboard themes and all three layouts work without clipping.
- [ ] Custom / Night Shift / Focus / Cinematic / Original Appearance presets work and individual controls restore in Custom.
- [ ] Rain, haze, neon signs, rail movement and reflections animate; toggles and intensity sliders work.
- [ ] Eco mode and Reduced Motion behave as intended.
- [ ] Compared with v0.4, CPU/GPU/RAM and fan noise are acceptable during normal use.
- [ ] Fullscreen games / VirtualBox pause policy and Windows battery policy work.
- [ ] Wallpaper resumes after sleep, display changes and Wallpaper Engine restart.
- [ ] Custom settings persist after restarting Wallpaper Engine.
- [ ] At least one extended normal session (30+ minutes) shows no visible freeze, audio runaway, excessive fan use or unexpected display issues.

**Recording results:** Mark PASS / FAIL / NOT TESTED for each check. Don't assume a blank check is a failure. If any issue appears, capture screenshots plus Wallpaper Engine Properties/diagnostics and compare with v0.4 under similar conditions.
